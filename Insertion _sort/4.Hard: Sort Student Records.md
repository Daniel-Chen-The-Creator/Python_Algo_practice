Exercise 4 — Hard: Sort Student Records

Each record below contains a student's name, score, and age, in that order:

students = [
    ["Amy", 85, 19],
    ["Ben", 92, 20],
    ["Cara", 85, 18],
    ["Dan", 92, 18],
    ["Eva", 85, 18],
    ["Finn", 78, 19]
]

Use insertion sort with these rules:

Students with higher scores come first.

If scores are equal, younger students come first.

If both score and age are equal, preserve the original order.

Expected Result

[
    ["Dan", 92, 18],
    ["Ben", 92, 20],
    ["Cara", 85, 18],
    ["Eva", 85, 18],
    ["Amy", 85, 19],
    ["Finn", 78, 19]
]

Requirements

Modify the original list in place.

Explain why Cara remains before Eva after sorting.

Hint: In each pass, key is an entire student record. Decide whether the record to its left should move one position to the right.
