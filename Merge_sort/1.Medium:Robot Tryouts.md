Problem 1: Robot Tryouts

Difficulty: Medium

Problem Description

The University of Toronto’s robotics club is selecting students for an upcoming competition.

There are \(N\) applicants. Each applicant has a unique registration number from \(1\) to \(N\), assigned in the order they registered.

Each student completes a test and receives a score. The club also records the time the student took to complete the test.

The students are ranked using the following rules:

A student with a higher score is ranked first.
If two students have the same score, the student with the shorter completion time is ranked first.
If both their scores and completion times are equal, the student who registered earlier is ranked first.

Determine the final ranking of all students.

Input Specification

The first line contains an integer \(N\), the number of students.

The next \(N\) lines each contain two integers. The \(i\)-th of these lines contains \(S_i\) and \(T_i\), the score and completion time in seconds of the student with registration number \(i\).

The following constraints apply:

$$ 1 \le N \le 200\,000 $$ $$ 0 \le S_i \le 100 $$ $$ 1 \le T_i \le 10^9 $$
Output Specification

Output one line containing the \(N\) registration numbers, separated by spaces, in order from highest-ranked to lowest-ranked.

Sample Input
6
90 120
95 150
90 100
95 150
90 100
80 90
Sample Output
2 4 3 5 1 6
Explanation of Sample Output

Students 2 and 4 have the highest score. Their completion times are equal, so student 2 is ranked first because they registered earlier.

Students 1, 3, and 5 each scored 90. Students 3 and 5 finished faster than student 1. Student 3 registered before student 5, so these three students are ordered as 3 5 1.

Student 6 has the lowest score and is ranked last.
